# CraftHub 🚀

> A capability-driven ecosystem connecting skills, learning, projects, opportunities, research, infrastructure, and collaboration.

## 🌐 Overview

CraftHub is designed as a unified ecosystem for discovering, developing, demonstrating, and connecting capabilities.

Instead of focusing only on jobs or professional networking, CraftHub brings together the journey from learning and skill development to real-world projects, contributions, opportunities, research, and collaboration.

## 🎯 Vision

Build a platform where people and organizations can discover not only who someone is, but also:

- What they can do
- What they are learning
- What they have built
- What they have contributed to
- What they can improve
- What opportunities fit their capabilities
- What resources and infrastructure are available
- Who they can collaborate with

## ✨ Core Areas

### 👤 Profiles & Capabilities

Capability-focused profiles representing skills, experience, projects, achievements, learning progress, and evidence of work.

### 📚 Learning

Personalized learning paths designed around skill development, identified gaps, projects, and future opportunities.

### 🛠️ Projects

A space to create, discover, participate in, and showcase real-world projects and contributions.

### 💼 Opportunities

Connect people with internships, projects, research opportunities, professional opportunities, and organizational needs based on relevant capabilities.

### 🔬 Research

A dedicated ecosystem for research discovery, collaboration, knowledge sharing, and future intelligence-driven analysis.

### 🏭 Infrastructure

Discover physical capabilities such as laboratories, fabrication facilities, testing equipment, machines, and prototyping infrastructure.

### 🌐 Community

A capability-oriented professional community for sharing projects, achievements, technical work, learning progress, images, videos, and updates.

### 🧠 Intelligence

A future intelligence layer intended to support recommendations, learning guidance, opportunity discovery, research assistance, scenario analysis, and ecosystem insights.
