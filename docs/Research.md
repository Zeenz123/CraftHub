# CraftHub Infrastructure Exchange

**From Fragmented Capacity to Demand-Driven Innovation Infrastructure**

Research and evidence package for **CraftHub / Synergy Squad — Smart India Hackathon 2026**.

## Core proposition

CraftHub Infrastructure Exchange is proposed as a cross-sector demand-and-execution layer connecting project requirements with distributed physical innovation capacity:

**PROJECT / IDEA → TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS → STANDARDIZED PHYSICAL-WORK REQUEST → CAPABILITY + LOCATION + CAPACITY MATCHING → PROVIDER / QUOTE / SCHEDULE → PHYSICAL EXECUTION → RESULT + EVIDENCE → NEXT ITERATION / MORE DEMAND**

The concept is deliberately positioned as more than a laboratory booking platform. It aims to connect demand across students, innovators, startups, researchers, MSMEs and businesses with public, private and institutional laboratories, testing facilities, fabrication centres, machines and manufacturing capacity.

## Evidence discipline

This research does **not** claim that a specific percentage of Indian laboratories is idle, nor that a specific percentage lies outside I-STEM. The report identifies those figures as requiring a harmonized cross-dataset measurement.

The proposed capacity-expansion mechanism is a **testable hypothesis**:

> Fragmented and uncertain demand may make specialized infrastructure investment less attractive; aggregated recurring demand could provide stronger signals for existing providers to expand capacity or for new providers to emerge.

## Proposed pilot

Start with one geographic innovation cluster and measure:

- provider supply and available capacity
- standardized demand
- request-to-provider/quote time
- matching rate
- execution and turnaround
- provider utilization before/after participation
- incremental revenue from available capacity
- prototype/testing/pilot completion
- recurring unmet requirements
- new providers, machines or services added after sustained demand signals

## Intended use

This repository is a research/evidence package supporting CraftHub's SIH 2026 concept. It is not a claim that the proposed national infrastructure exchange has already been deployed or validated at national scale.
